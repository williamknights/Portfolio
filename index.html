Background

About Me

My professional background has consistently required translating process-heavy information into clear, actionable writing that helps real people do their jobs more confidently and efficiently.

Experienced in creating onboarding and service center documentation for office staff navigating software tools and internal systems. I have written guides, standard operating procedures, and industry reference materials covering various products and terminology to turn unfamiliar systems into something any new hire could follow on day one. I have learned how to study a tool, understand how a user interacts with it, and write documentation that closes the gap between the two.

I also spent time as a high school English teacher, where I learned to meet students where they are, breaking down dense or unfamiliar material into something clear and usable regardless of the audience's starting point. That instinct for the student carries directly into every document I write.

My writing is rooted in precision, empathy for the reader, and a deep respect for clarity. This portfolio showcases documentation and writing projects that reflect those values.
